For a subagent working beside others: ask for files outside your scope before changing them. Granted at once when no other subagent holds them, and then you may change them; otherwise the result says which subagent holds them and what it is doing. Then either call again with `wait: true`, which returns once that subagent has finished, or leave those files and say in your reply what is left for them. A wait that could never end (the holder is waiting on files you hold) is refused at once. Paths are relative to the working directory; a directory covers everything under it, and `*` matches any run of characters.
