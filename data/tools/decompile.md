Decompile a compiled binary to readable C pseudocode with kuna, far clearer than raw disassembly for reverse engineering. Give `binary` alone to list its functions, then call again with `function` (a name like `main`, or a `0x` address) for that function's pseudocode. Works on stripped binaries. If kuna is not installed, the result says how to get it; objdump disassembly is the fallback.
